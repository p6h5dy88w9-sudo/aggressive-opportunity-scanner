How the score works

100-point aggressive-growth model: trend, 20/60-day momentum, breakout proximity, relative volume, market-cap tier, liquidity and volatility. V6 uses a broad 35-day screen first and requests 90-day history plus market cap only for the strongest survivors. Higher scores mean stronger setup characteristics—not guaranteed returns. It is a screening model—not a prediction or a buy signal.
