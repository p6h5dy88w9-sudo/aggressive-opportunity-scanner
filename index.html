How V8 works

Stage 1 first applies the selected market-cap range, then ranks same-day price change, relative volume when available, dollar liquidity and cap-tier activity across the loaded snapshot without using the API. Stage 2 makes one 90-day history request per finalist, never uses a Basic-incompatible batch request, waits between requests, and caches every successful symbol. Pausing or reopening V8 lets the deep scan resume without repeating cached history. Market cap from the snapshot is reused; V8 does not spend extra Basic-plan credits trying to fill missing market caps.

Portfolio guardrails

  • Use the scanner to generate candidates, not automatic buy orders.
  • Verify earnings, news, valuation and thesis before buying.
  • Prefer liquid names and defined risk for options.
  • Cap speculative positions so one loss cannot dominate the account.
