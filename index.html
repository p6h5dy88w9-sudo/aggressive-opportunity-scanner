How V7 works

Stage 1 is local and market-wide when a bulk snapshot is loaded: V7 ranks same-day price change, relative volume, dollar liquidity and small/micro-cap activity without downloading history for every symbol. Stage 2 requests 90-day history only for the highest-ranked finalists, then scores trend, 20/60-day momentum, breakout proximity, relative volume, market-cap tier, liquidity and volatility. Market cap from the snapshot is reused when available, avoiding another API call.

Portfolio guardrails

  • Use the scanner to generate candidates, not automatic buy orders.
  • Verify earnings, news, valuation and thesis before buying.
  • Prefer liquid names and defined risk for options.
  • Cap speculative positions so one loss cannot dominate the account.
